FAQs

What is Screener AI? How useful is it?

Screener AI is the assistant you always wanted. It is hard-working, sincere, and very intelligent. You ask it a question, and it reads through documents to answer your query. We use it a lot to co-work on an investment idea. Sometimes we ask it to compile data from the documents, while other times we ask it to explain things to us in simple language. It can help you understand the companies better. It is pretty useful.

How is Screener AI different from other AI tools?

All other tools, including ChatGPT Pro and Perplexity, use search engines to find relevant documents. They often miss a lot of relevant documents in this approach. This is more error-prone, costly, and time-consuming.

Screener AI has direct access to the official filings of the companies. Plus, we have innovated lots of new technologies that improve the understanding of the official documents. This gives much better results – almost like an expert.

Is Screener AI paid? What is the pricing?

Yes, Screener AI is paid because of the underlying cost per token. You can do a top-up to use Screener AI for the amount you want. You can also purchase Screener Premium to get free AI Credits worth ₹500.

How much does each answer cost?

Average cost comes to around ₹10-30 per answer. The cost depends on the number of documents AI reads. It can even go up to ₹100 if the AI reads a lot of documents. At the raw level, it is ₹100 per million input tokens and ₹800 per million output tokens.

How can I reduce the costs?

We understand that the costs of answers are quite high sometimes. This is because of the high-intelligence models we use. You can switch to the "General Intelligence" mode for much lower costs. We are also constantly working on new ways to reduce this cost. Give us some time.

How good is it?

We have been using Screener AI internally for 2 months before launching. We love the answers it gives. It understands our queries and gives insights which help us make better decisions. It is pretty good. Try it!

How do I use Screener AI?

Screener AI is currently available on the company pages. Open any company, click on the AI button at the top. It will open a chat window where you can ask your queries about the company.
