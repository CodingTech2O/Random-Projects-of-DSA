Enhanced Ratio Library

April 2026

Upgraded Cash Flow Statement with Free Cash Flow & CFO/OP Ratio

We have added two new meaningful metrics to the Cash Flow statement:

1) Free Cash Flow: This shows how much of a cash a company generates after covering its operating expenses and capital expenditures

2) CFO/OP Ratio: This measures how much of the reported profit turns into cash.
