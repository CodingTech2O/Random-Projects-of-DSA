Key Points

Business Profile [1]
Established in 1981, Frontier Springs Limited is a certified manufacturer specializing in hot-coiled compression springs, air springs, and forged components primarily for Indian Railways. The company supplies components for wagons, carriages, and locomotives, including India's fastest train, the Vande Bharat Express. The company primarily caters to Indian Railways and has been registered with RDSO for 30+ years.
