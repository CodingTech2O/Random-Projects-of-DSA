About The Course

Welcome to the How to use screener.in course on Upsurge.club, where you will learn how to effectively use screener.in for picking & analyzing the right stock. You will also learn to use screener.in for reading financial statements and discover some hidden benefits and features, all supporting your endeavour of becoming a better investor.

In Lesson 1, Introduction , you will receive a comprehensive overview of the Screener platform, gaining insights into its functionalities and features.

In Lesson 2, Reading Financial Statements , we will delve into the intricacies of interpreting financial statements – balance sheets, profit & loss statements, and cash flow statements – using the powerful tool, Screener. You will also discover how to make insightful notes and leverage Screener's capabilities to export data seamlessly into Excel for ratio analysis and advanced financial modeling.

In Lesson 3, Using screener.in to identify multibagger Stocks & Ratio Analysis , we will delve into the process of creating screens on Screener, exploring both the fundamentals of screen creation and examining some widely-used screens. Additionally, we will navigate through the essential ratios available on Screener, learning how to customize them to align with our specific requirements.

In Lesson 4, Hidden Features & Benefits of screener.in , we will learn to create a watchlist and learn how to track commodity prices. You will also learn the method of tracking the investments of ace investors. Finally, we will also explore some hidden features of Screener.

Course highlights:
